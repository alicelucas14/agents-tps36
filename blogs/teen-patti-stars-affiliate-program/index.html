What Exactly Is the Teen Patti Stars Affiliate Program?

It is a network-style referral program. You download the app (or visit the official site), get your exclusive referral link or code, and share it with friends, family, or online audiences. When someone downloads the game through your link, registers, and starts playing, they become part of your downline.

Earnings come mainly from a “negative profit” model. In simple language: the platform looks at the money lost by the players under you, subtracts certain costs, and shares a percentage with you. Official examples show calculations like this:

  • If subordinate players lose ₹1,000 total and costs are ₹200, with a 50% sharing ratio, the agent earns ₹400.

Higher activity and larger networks can increase earnings. The program promotes unlimited earning potential as your downline grows, plus instant invite bonuses and lifetime-style commissions on player activity in many promotional messages.

In addition to the more organized Big Agent Plan targeted at serious marketers, the program offers a basic Refer & Earn option for smaller-scale invitations. 

Important note for India: The service is not available in Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Odisha (Orissa), and Nagaland. Players must be 18+. Always check current legal status in your state, as online real-money gaming rules can vary.

No. Many people begin purely through personal networks and social media. A website or larger audience helps scale later.

What if a referred player never deposits?

You generally earn from actual activity and losses (or deposits in some bonus structures), not just installs.
