What is Teen Patti?

Teen Patti, originating from the Indian subcontinent, is a simplified version of poker. The game’s rules are easy to grasp, making it ideal for casual players looking to enjoy a quick match. Typically, the game is played with a standard 52-card deck, and each player is dealt three cards. The objective is to have the best hand, but unlike other poker variants, the rankings in Teen Patti vary slightly, adding to its charm.
