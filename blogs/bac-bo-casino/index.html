How to Play Bac Bo

Basic Rules

  1. Dice Rolls: Bac Bo uses four dice – two red dice for the player and two blue dice for the banker.
  2. Betting Options: Players can bet on three outcomes: Player Wins, Banker Wins, or Tie.
  3. Rolling Dice: The dice are rolled, and the results are displayed. The sum of the player’s dice is compared to the sum of the banker’s dice.
  4. Determining the Winner: The side with the higher total wins. If both totals are the same, it’s a tie.

Betting Options and Payouts

  • Player Wins: A bet on the player wins if the player’s total is higher.
  • Banker Wins: A bet on the banker wins if the banker’s total is higher.
