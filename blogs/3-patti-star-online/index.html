The Different Variations of 3 Patti Star Online

One of the unique aspects of 3 Patti Star is its diversity in gameplay variations:

Winning Hands and Their Rankings

In 3 Patti Star, hand rankings are fundamental to the game:

  • High Card: The simplest form with no pairs.
  • Pair and Trail: Include matching cards with a trail as the highest level.
