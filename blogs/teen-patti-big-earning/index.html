Understanding the Basics

Rules of Teen Patti

Three face-down cards are dealt to each player in Teen Patti. The boot amount, or the smallest stake put in the pot, is used to start the game. Players then take turns betting, with the option to see their cards (seen) or keep them face down (blind). The game continues until a showdown, where the player with the best hand wins the pot.

Hand Rankings

The hand rankings in Teen Patti, from highest to lowest, include:

  • Trail (three of a kind)
  • Pure Sequence (straight flush)
  • Sequence (straight)
  • Color (flush)
  • Pair (two of a kind)
  • High Card
