Teen Patti Casino Card Rankings: Which Hands Beat Which?

September 12, 2026·by Rosh·Blog

If you’ve ever sat around a table in India during Diwali, Holi, or a late-night session with friends and felt that split-second confusion about whose three cards actually win, this guide is for you. Teen Patti (also called 3 Patti or Flush) is pure Indian card culture, and knowing the exact teen patti casino card rankings separates the confident players from the ones who keep folding good hands or calling bad ones.

Here’s the clear, no-nonsense order used in most home games and online casinos popular with Indian players. Category always comes first. A weak hand in a higher category beats the strongest hand in a lower one.

The Complete Ranking (Highest to Lowest)

  1. Trail / Trio / Set (Three of a Kind)
    Three cards of the same rank.
    Best: A-A-A
    Worst: 2-2-2
    This is the boss. Even the lowest Trail (three 2s) beats every other hand type.
  2. Pure Sequence (Straight Flush / Pakka Sequence)
    Three consecutive cards of the same suit.
    Common high examples: A-K-Q of the same suit or A-2-3 of the same suit (house rules sometimes differ on which ranks higher).
    Lowest usually: 4-3-2 same suit.
    Note: A-K-2 is never a sequence.
  3. Sequence (Run / Straight)
    Three consecutive cards of mixed suits.
    Same ranking order as Pure Sequence.
    A Sequence always beats a Color, even if the Color has higher individual cards.
  4. Color (Flush)
    Three cards of the same suit that are not consecutive.
    Compared by highest card, then second, then third.
    Example: A-K-7 of hearts beats A-Q-10 of spades.
  5. PairTwo cards of the same rank + one kicker.
    Higher pair wins. If pairs are equal, the kicker decides.
    Best: A-A-K
    Worst: 2-2-3
  6. High CardNothing matches. No pair, no sequence, no color.
    Highest card first, then second, then third.
    A-K-J mixed suits is strong here; 5-3-2 is weak.

Quick “What Beats What” Memory Aid

  • Trail beats Pure Sequence
  • Pure Sequence beats Sequence
  • Sequence beats Color
  • Color beats Pair
  • Pair beats High Card

This is the single most useful thing to remember. Many new players assume Color (same suit) should rank above Sequence because it “looks prettier.” In Teen Patti it does not. Sequence is rarer in the three-card format, so it ranks higher.

How Ties Are Broken

When two players have the same category:

  • Trail vs Trail: Higher rank wins (Aces beat Kings, etc.). Identical Trails are almost impossible with one deck.
  • Pure Sequence / Sequence: Higher top card (or the accepted A-2-3 / A-K-Q order of the table).
  • Color: Highest card, then next, then lowest.
  • Pair: Higher pair, then higher kicker.
  • High Card: Highest card, then second, then third.

Some tables also use suit order (Spades > Hearts > Diamonds > Clubs) as a final tiebreaker, but this is not universal. Always confirm the house rule before the first hand.

Why These Rankings Feel Different from Poker

In five-card poker a Straight Flush is rarer and ranks above Three of a Kind. In three-card Teen Patti the probabilities flip slightly, and the cultural ranking (inherited from older Brag-style games) puts Trail on top. Online casinos popular among Indian players almost always stick to the Trail > Pure Sequence > Sequence > Color > Pair > High Card order.

Practical Tips for Indian Players

  • At family gatherings or local clubs, the rules are usually the classic ones above. Online apps and casinos sometimes highlight “A-2-3 pure sequence” as special or use slightly different sequence top ranks—check the info button.
  • Sequence vs Color is the most common argument. Memorize it once and you’ll avoid 90% of table disputes.
  • Bluffing works better when you understand relative strength. A mid-range Sequence is stronger than many people realize.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Trail always beat Pure Sequence?

Yes. Three 2s beat A-K-Q of the same suit.

Is A-2-3 a valid sequence?

Yes, and many tables treat it as the highest sequence. A-K-2 is never valid.

Does Color beat Sequence?

No. Sequence ranks higher.

What is the strongest possible hand?

A-A-A (Trail of Aces).

Are the rankings the same in online Teen Patti casinos in India?

Yes for the vast majority of legitimate platforms. Variations like Muflis reverse the entire order (lowest wins), but standard tables follow the ranking above.
