Available Payment Methods for Teen Patti Stars Deposit in India

This is the clear favourite for most players. UPI deposits are fast, require no extra cards, and work straight from your linked bank account. Paytm

You can use either the Paytm wallet or Paytm UPI. Enter your Paytm-linked number, receive an OTP if needed, and confirm. Many players like this for smaller or mid-sized deposits because it feels familiar and reliable.

PhonePe and Google Pay

Both work through the UPI route or as wallet options where supported. These are excellent if you already keep money in these apps. Instant confirmation is the norm when the network is stable.

Useful for larger amounts or when you want a clear bank record. IMPS is faster than NEFT. It may take a few minutes longer than pure UPI, but it is reliable and trackable.

Other supporting options that occasionally appear include additional e-wallets. Always check the live deposit page inside the app, as available methods can vary slightly based on your location and current payment partner configurations.
