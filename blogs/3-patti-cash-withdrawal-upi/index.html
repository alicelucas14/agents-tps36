Understanding UPI in India

What is UPI (Unified Payments Interface)?

UPI is a real-time payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI), enabling instant bank transfers across different platforms. With just a UPI ID, users can make transactions without entering sensitive bank details repeatedly.

The Rise of UPI as a Preferred Payment Method

Since its inception, UPI has revolutionized payments in India, providing a secure, streamlined, and convenient way to handle money. Its popularity is owed to the simplicity of transactions and the extensive network of banks supporting it.
