Types of Teen Patti Offers

Welcome Bonuses

Welcome bonuses are one of the most common offers extended by online platforms. These bonuses are designed to attract new players, offering them a head start with extra funds or free chips to play. Typically, these bonuses are awarded upon signing up and making an initial deposit. They provide an excellent opportunity to explore the game without risking too much of your own money.

Deposit Match Offers

Deposit match offers are a favorite among many players. These offers provide a percentage match on your deposit, effectively doubling or even tripling your playing funds. For instance, a 100% deposit match means that if you deposit ₹1000, you get an additional ₹1000 to play with. This boosts your bankroll, allowing for extended playtime and more chances to win. However, it’s essential to read the terms and conditions carefully to understand wagering requirements and withdrawal limits.

No-Deposit Bonuses

No-deposit bonuses are rare but highly sought after. These incentives, as their name implies, don’t require a deposit. Players receive a small amount of money or free chips just for signing up. Referral Bonuses

Referral bonuses are a win-win for both the referring player and the new player. By inviting friends to join the platform, you can earn bonus chips or cash rewards. Cashback Offers

Cashback offers are a form of insurance against losses. Players receive a percentage of their losses back, usually as bonus chips or credits. Cashback offers vary in percentage and frequency, so it’s wise to choose platforms that provide consistent and generous cashback deals.
