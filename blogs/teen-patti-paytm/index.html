Understanding Teen Patti Gameplay

Basic Rules of Teen Patti

Teen Patti is played with a standard 52-card deck, excluding jokers. Here are the basic rules:

  • Each player is dealt three cards face down.
  • Three players is the minimum required to play the game, while 10 players is the maximum.
  • Players bet based on the strength of their cards, similar to Poker.

Variations of the Game

Teen Patti has several exciting variations:

  • Muflis (Lowball): The weakest hand wins.
  • Joker: Some cards are designated as jokers, adding a twist.
  • AK47: Aces, Kings, Fours, and Sevens are treated as jokers.
  • 999: The hand closest to 999 wins.
