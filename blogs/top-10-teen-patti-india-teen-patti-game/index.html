FAQs on Teen Patti Game

What is Teen Patti, and how is it played?

Teen Patti, often called “Indian Poker,” is a popular card game in India where players aim to form the best three-card hand. The game starts with players placing bets, followed by each receiving three cards. The goal is to have the highest-ranking hand according to Teen Patti rules, which include sequences like pure sequence, sequence, color, pair, and high card. Players can either fold or raise bets as the game progresses.

What are the variations of Teen Patti?

Teen Patti has several variations, making it more exciting. Some popular versions include Muflis (where the weakest hand wins), AK47 (where aces, kings, 4s, and 7s are wild cards), Joker, and Best of Four (players are dealt four cards and choose the best three). These variations add different layers of strategy to the traditional game.
