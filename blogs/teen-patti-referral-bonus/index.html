FAQs on Teen Patti Referral Bonus

What is a referral bonus in Teen Patti, and how does it work?

A referral bonus in Teen Patti is a reward given to players who invite others to join the platform using their unique referral code or link. When your referee signs up and meets certain conditions, such as depositing money or playing a specific number of games, both you and your referee receive rewards.

Can I withdraw the referral bonus directly?

In most cases, referral bonuses cannot be withdrawn directly. They may need to be used for in-game play or meet specific wagering requirements before they become eligible for withdrawal.

What happens if my friend doesn’t use my referral code during sign-up?

You may not obtain the bonus if your friend forgets to use your referral code. Encourage referees to double-check that they have entered the correct code during registration.

Do referral bonuses expire?

Yes, many referral bonuses have an expiration date. If you don’t claim or use the bonus within the specified period, it may be forfeited. Check the platform’s terms to avoid losing your rewards.
