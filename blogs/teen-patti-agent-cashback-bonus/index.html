Understanding Teen Patti

What is Teen Patti?

Teen Patti, meaning “three cards” in Hindi, is a traditional Indian card game similar to poker. Played with a standard 52-card deck, the game aims to have the best three-card hand or to bluff your way to victory.

Basic Rules and Gameplay

The game starts with players placing an initial bet, known as the boot amount. Each player receives three cards face down. Players then take turns to call (match the previous bet), raise (increase the bet), or fold (withdraw from the round). The goal is to either have the highest-ranking hand or to be the last player standing after others have folded.
