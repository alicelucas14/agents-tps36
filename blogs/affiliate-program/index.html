Understanding Teen Patti

History and Origin

Teen Patti, often referred to as the Indian version of Poker, has its roots deeply embedded in Indian culture. Traditionally played during festivals and social gatherings, Teen Patti has now found a massive audience online, making it a popular choice among card game aficionados worldwide.

How to Play Teen Patti

The game is simple yet exciting. It involves a standard 52-card deck, excluding jokers. The object of the game is to have the best three-card hand once each player is dealt three cards. The game includes betting rounds, and the player with the highest hand ranking wins. It’s this blend of strategy and luck that makes Teen Patti so addictive and entertaining.

What is an Affiliate Program?

Definition and Concept

An online shop pays a commission to an external website for traffic or purchases that are produced from referrals, and this arrangement is known as an affiliate program. Essentially, affiliates promote the retailer’s products or services and earn a percentage of the revenue generated from their marketing efforts.

How Affiliate Programs Work

Affiliates join a program, receive unique tracking links, and use these links to drive traffic to the retailer’s site. When a user clicks on the link and completes a desired action (like making a purchase), the affiliate earns a commission. It’s a win-win situation for both the affiliate and the retailer.
