What is Teen Patti?

Teen Patti, meaning “three cards” in Hindi, originated in India and has grown in popularity across South Asia. It’s a game that combines elements of skill and chance, similar to poker. Players bet on who has the best three-card hand, and the goal is to maximize the pot before revealing the cards.

Basic Rules of the Game

The game starts with a dealer distributing three cards to each player. Players can choose to play blind (bet without looking at their cards) or seen (bet after seeing their cards). The rounds continue until all but one player folds or the final call is made. The pot is shared by the player with the better hand. Hands are ranked similarly to poker, with sequences like a trail (three of a kind), pure sequence (straight flush), and so on.
