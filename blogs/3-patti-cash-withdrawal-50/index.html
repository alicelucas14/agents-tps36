What is 3 Patti?

3 Patti, also known as Teen Patti, is a beloved card game originating from India. Its rich history dates back centuries, making it a staple in Indian culture, especially during festivals and gatherings.

History and Origins

3 Patti is derived from the English game of three-card brag and shares similarities with poker. Over time, it has evolved into a game enjoyed by people of all ages, both in homes and online.

Basic Rules and Gameplay

The game involves three cards dealt to each player. Having the best hand or bluffing your way to victory is the aim of the game. Hands are ranked from high to low, with the highest being a trail (three of a kind) and the lowest being a high card.
