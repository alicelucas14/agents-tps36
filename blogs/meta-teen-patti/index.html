Game Variations in Meta Teen Patti

Classic Mode

Classic Mode adheres to the traditional rules of Teen Patti. It’s ideal for players who prefer the original gameplay without additional twists or modifications.

Joker Mode

In Joker Mode, joker cards act as wild cards, creating opportunities for unique hand combinations and strategies. This mode adds an element of surprise and excitement to the game.

Muflis Mode

Muflis Mode reverses the hand rankings, making the lowest hand the highest. This variation challenges players to rethink their strategies and adapt to the new dynamics.

AK47 Mode

AK47 Mode introduces specific cards as wild cards (Aces, Kings, Fours, and Sevens), adding complexity and strategic depth. This mode requires players to leverage these wild cards effectively.

Highest and Lowest Mode

In Highest and Lowest Mode, players aim for either the highest or the lowest hand. This dual objective mode tests players’ versatility and adaptability.
