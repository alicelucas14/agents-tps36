What Is the Teen Patti Stars Agent Commission?

Teen Patti Stars runs a referral-based agent (also called Big Agent or Elite Agent) program. You get a unique invite link or code. When new players join and play through your link, they become your downline. You then earn commission based on their activity—mainly tied to the net losses (or “negative profit”) of those players after certain platform costs.

Official materials highlight earnings of up to 70% commission in the referral/agent setup, lifetime commission potential on active players, instant invite bonuses, and regular (often weekly) payouts. The more active players you bring and the more they play, the higher your earnings can grow. Many agents treat it as a side income that can scale with network size.

How the Commission Is Calculated

The core formula shared by the platform is a negative-profit (or negative-profile) sharing model:

(Total money lost by all your subordinate players − three platform costs) × negative profit sharing ratio

Simple example from official explanations:

If your downline players lose a total of ₹1,000 and the three costs total ₹200, with a 50% sharing ratio, your commission is:

(₹1,000 − ₹200) × 50% = ₹400.

The exact ratio and cost deductions can vary by agent level or current program rules, and higher-tier agents often get better rates or additional bonuses. Commission is typically ongoing (lifetime on qualified activity of your players) rather than a one-time signup fee. There are also milestone or invite bonuses layered on top—instant rewards for bringing new players and tiered bonuses as your active team grows.

Payouts are commonly processed weekly (frequently highlighted on weekends, with specific claim slots such as afternoon windows). Earnings appear in your agent wallet or mailbox after system checks. Larger amounts may involve verification steps.

Step-by-Step: How to Become an Agent and Start Earning

  1. Download the official Teen Patti Stars app (from the platform’s channels) and create/register your account.
  2. Locate the agent / Big Agent / referral / invite section inside the app or on the dedicated agent portal.
  3. Generate your unique referral link or code.
  4. Share it with friends, family, or contacts via WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Facebook groups, or local gaming communities.
  5. Track new sign-ups and activity through the in-app agent dashboard. New players who download and log in via your link automatically join your downline.
  6. Encourage healthy, ongoing play (the commission grows with real activity, not just installs).
  7. Claim commissions during the announced payout windows and withdraw via supported methods (UPI and other common Indian options are typically available).

No special “office” or heavy investment is required—many people run it from home. Success depends on consistent sharing and keeping your network active.
