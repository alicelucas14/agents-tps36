Introduction to Fan Tan Live

Understanding the Basics of Fan Tan Live

The objective of Fan Tan Live is straightforward: predict the number of beads left on the table after they are divided by four. Key terminology includes terms like “tan,” “sheh-sam-hong,” and “nig-nang.”

Rules of Fan Tan Live

Fan Tan Live requires minimal equipment: a cup, a dealer’s stick, and numerous small beads or buttons. The game flow involves players placing bets, the dealer revealing the beads, and the final count determining the winner.

How to Play Fan Tan Live

Players start by placing bets on possible outcomes. The dealer then uses a cup to collect and reveal a random number of beads. The beads are counted in groups of four until the remainder is determined, identifying the winning bet.
