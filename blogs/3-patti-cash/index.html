How to Play 3 Patti Cash

Basic Rules

  1. Players: 3-7 players can participate.
  2. Deck: Jokers are not used, and a conventional 52-card deck is utilized.
  3. Objective: Create a higher-ranking hand than your opponents to win the pot.

Gameplay Steps

  1. Ante or Boot Amount: All players contribute a fixed amount to the pot before the game begins.
  2. Player Actions: Players can choose to play as blind (without seeing their cards) or seen (after viewing their cards).
    • Blind: Bet without looking at the cards.
    • Seen: Bet after viewing your cards, but the stake is higher.
  3. Showdown: When two players remain, a showdown occurs, revealing their cards to determine the winner.

Hand Rankings in 3 Patti Cash

To become an expert player, you must comprehend hand rankings. Here are the standings, presented from top to bottom:

  1. Trail (Set): Three identically ranked cards are A♥ A♦ A♣.
  2. Pure Sequence (Straight Flush): Three consecutive cards of the same suit (e.g., Q♠ K♠ A♠).
  3. Sequence (Straight): Three consecutive cards of different suits (e.g., 4♥ 5♣ 6♦).
  4. Color (Flush): Three identical cards, but not in the same order (for example, 2♠ 6♠ 10♠).
  5. Pair: Two cards of the same rank (e.g., 9♦ 9♠ K♣).
  6. High Card: The highest card available, in the event that no other combinations are made.
