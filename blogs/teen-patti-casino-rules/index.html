What Exactly Is Teen Patti?

Teen Patti (also called 3 Patti) is a three-card game played with a standard 52-card deck (no jokers in the classic version). It usually involves 3 to 6 or 7 players. The goal is simple: either hold the strongest three-card hand or force everyone else to fold so you take the pot.

It feels a bit like poker but moves faster and has its own Indian flavour — especially the choice between playing “blind” or “seen.”

Basic Setup

  • Everyone puts a small fixed amount into the pot first. This is called the boot or ante.
  • The dealer shuffles and gives each player three cards face down.
  • Play moves clockwise, starting from the player to the left of the dealer.
  • The dealer position usually rotates after every hand.

That’s it. No complicated board cards or long streets like in Texas Hold’em.

Teen Patti Hand Rankings (From Strongest to Weakest)

Memorise this order — it’s the heart of the game:

  1. Trail / Trio – Three cards of the same rank (A-A-A is the best, 2-2-2 is the lowest).
  2. Pure Sequence – Three consecutive cards of the same suit (for example A-K-Q of hearts or 5-4-3 of clubs).
  3. Sequence – Three consecutive cards of mixed suits (9-8-7 of different suits).
  4. Colour – Three cards of the same suit that are not in sequence (K-J-7 of diamonds).
  5. Pair – Two cards of the same rank plus one kicker (Q-Q-5).
  6. High Card – Nothing matching; the highest card decides (A-J-8 beats K-10-4).

A weak sequence still beats even a strong colour. Many beginners get this mixed up, so keep the ranking order handy the first few times you play.

Blind vs Seen – The Key Decision

This is what makes Teen Patti special:

  • Blind – You bet without looking at your cards. You only need to put in the minimum stake (usually equal to the current boot level).
  • Seen – You look at your cards. Once you see them, you must bet at least double what a blind player would put in.

You can start blind and decide to see your cards later on your turn. After that, you become a seen player for the rest of the hand.

Betting Actions You’ll Use

On your turn you can:

  • Pack / Fold – Throw your cards and leave the hand. You lose whatever you already put in.
  • Chaal / Call – Match the current required amount and stay in.
  • Raise – Increase the bet (within the table limits). This forces others to put in more money if they want to continue.
  • Side Show – Available when there are three or more players left and both you and the previous player are seen. You ask the previous player to compare cards privately. The weaker hand packs. The other player can refuse the side show.
  • Show – When only two players remain, either can ask for a show. Both cards are revealed and the better hand wins the pot. Rules about who can demand a show sometimes differ slightly between tables (blind vs seen), so check the house rules.

Betting continues until only one player is left or a show happens.

How a Typical Hand Plays Out

  1. Everyone puts the boot.
  2. Cards are dealt.
  3. First player decides blind or seen and acts.
  4. Action goes around the table. Players pack, chaal, or raise.
  5. Side shows may happen if allowed.
  6. Eventually one player takes the pot either by everyone else folding or by winning the final show.

Common Variations You’ll See in India

Classic Teen Patti is the base, but popular versions include:

  • Joker – One or more wild cards.
  • AK47 – Aces, Kings, 4s and 7s act as jokers.
  • Muflis – Lowest hand wins (completely reversed rankings).
  • Best of Four – You get four cards and choose the best three.

Online tables and home games often list which version is being played, so always check before the first hand.

Tips for Absolute Beginners

  • Learn the hand rankings first. Everything else becomes easier once you know what beats what.
  • Start at low-stakes tables or free-play modes.
  • Don’t feel forced to see your cards early. Playing blind can keep the pressure lower and sometimes forces stronger hands to fold.
  • Watch a few hands before jumping in if you’re on an online table.
  • Manage your money. Decide a limit before you sit and stick to it.
  • In India, laws around real-money online gaming can differ by state and have seen recent changes. Always check current local rules and play only on platforms that follow them.

Why Teen Patti Feels So Indian

The game mixes skill (reading people, knowing when to fold, understanding position) with the thrill of the unknown. The blind/seen choice and the side-show drama give it a social energy that pure poker tables sometimes miss. Whether you’re playing with cousins during the festive season or trying an online table from home, the core teen patti casino rules stay the same.

Once you play a few hands, the rhythm becomes natural. Focus on the rankings, respect the betting structure, and you’ll be ready to enjoy the game the way millions of Indians already do.
