Understanding the Game

Basic Rules

Lightning Dice is played with three standard six-sided dice. The primary objective is to predict the total sum of the dice after they are rolled.

Objective of the Game

Players place their bets on various possible outcomes, hoping to correctly guess the total sum of the dice to win payouts, which can be significantly boosted by lightning multipliers.

Gameplay Mechanics

The Dice Tower

A key feature of Lightning Dice is the Dice Tower. The dealer drops the dice into this tower, and they tumble through various obstacles before landing at the bottom, revealing the final outcome.

Lightning Multipliers

Before each roll, random lightning multipliers are applied to different potential outcomes. These multipliers can significantly increase the payouts for lucky players.

Players place their bets on the betting grid, choosing from a range of possible outcomes. Bets can be placed on individual numbers or combinations, each with its own payout odds.
