What Is the Gates of Olympus RTP?

Olympus RTP’s typical Gates is 96.50%. That means, over a very large number of spins, the game is designed to return ₹96.50 for every ₹100 wagered.

Pragmatic Play also offers lower configurations (commonly 95.51% and 94.50%). Many casinos choose one of these, so the first thing you should do is open the game’s info screen and check the exact RTP before you start spinning. A difference of 2% may not sound dramatic, but over hundreds of spins it adds up.

Volatility is high. Wins do not arrive evenly. You can go through long dry stretches and then hit a strong free-spins round that changes the session. This is normal for the game and something Indian players who prefer high-risk, high-reward slots already understand.

Maximum win is capped at 5,000x your stake. On a ₹20 spin that equals ₹1,00,000; on a ₹100 spin it reaches ₹5,00,000.

How Multipliers Work in Gates of Olympus

Zeus randomly throws multiplier orbs onto the 6×5 grid. These orbs carry values from 2x up to 500x. Multiple orbs on the same spin simply add together.

Only the current tumble sequence is affected by the multiplier in the base game before it resets. You can still see solid hits when a few higher orbs land together with a decent cluster of symbols (you need 8 or more matching symbols anywhere on the grid for a win).

The real power appears in free spins.

Free Spins – Where the Big Multipliers Build

Land 4 or more Zeus scatter symbols and you trigger free spins:

  • 4 scatters → 15 free spins + 3x bet payout
  • 5 scatters → 15 free spins + 5x bet payout
  • 6 scatters → 15 free spins + 100x bet payout

During free spins the multiplier system changes completely. For the duration of the feature, the value of each multiplier orb that lands on a winning tumble is permanently added to a running total multiplier.  That total never resets until the free spins end.

So an early 10x, followed later by a 25x and a 50x, leaves you with an 85x multiplier applying to every subsequent win. This is how the game produces its biggest results.

You can also retrigger. Landing 3 or more scatters during free spins awards an extra 5 spins, with no upper limit on how many times this can happen.

Extra Options You’ll See in India

  • Ante Bet: Increase your stake by 25% and roughly double the chance of triggering free spins. Useful if you want more frequent bonus rounds.
  • Bonus Buy: Where available, you can buy the free-spins feature for 100x your stake. The casino and regional regulations determine availability. 

Both features are popular among Indian players who prefer a more active style of play.

Practical Tips for Indian Players

  1. Always verify the RTP inside the game client.
  2. Manage your bankroll for high volatility — longer sessions need a bigger cushion.
  3. The tumble (cascade) feature means one good spin can chain into several wins, so don’t rush.
  4. Free spins are the main source of larger returns; the base game mostly keeps you in the session.
  5. Play on mobile if that’s more convenient — the game is fully optimised and widely available on Indian-facing casino apps and sites.
