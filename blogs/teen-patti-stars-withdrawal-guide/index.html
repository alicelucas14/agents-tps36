Prerequisites Before You Attempt Teen Patti Stars Withdrawal

Most successful withdrawals share these basics:

  • Account fully registered with a valid Indian mobile number.
  • KYC completed (usually Aadhaar + PAN, sometimes a selfie or cancelled cheque). Name, date of birth and bank/UPI details must match exactly.
  • Sufficient withdrawable balance (bonus money is usually locked until wagering conditions are met).
  • Payment details already linked and verified in your own name.
  • No pending security flags or incomplete previous requests.

Skipping KYC is the most common reason first-time withdrawals get stuck.

Step-by-Step Teen Patti Stars Withdrawal Process

The exact menu labels can vary slightly with app updates, but the flow is consistent:

  1. Open the Teen Patti Stars app and log in.
  2. Go to your Wallet / Balance / Cashier section (usually top right or bottom navigation).
  3. Tap Withdraw / Cash Out / Redeem.
  4. Choose your preferred method (UPI is the most popular and fastest for most Indian users).
  5. Enter the amount — stay within the minimum and maximum shown on screen and within your available (non-bonus) balance.
  6. Confirm or add your UPI ID / bank account details carefully. Double-check spelling, IFSC and account number.
  7. Review everything, enter any OTP if asked, and submit.
  8. Note the transaction / request ID and check the status in Transaction History or Records.

Once submitted, the request moves to processing. First withdrawals or larger amounts often take longer because of manual or automated verification checks.

Common Teen Patti Stars Withdrawal Issues and Fixes

Here are the problems Indian players report most often, with practical solutions:

  1. Withdrawal stuck on “Pending”

Usually KYC incomplete, first-time request, or system review. Wait 24–48 hours, then contact in-app support with your request ID. Do not submit multiple requests — it can create more flags.

  1. Request rejected or failed

Most common causes: wrong UPI/bank details, name mismatch with PAN/Aadhaar, or balance still under bonus wagering. Correct the details and try again with a small test amount first.

  1. Money taken out of the wallet but not received

Check transaction history. If the status is “successful” on the app side but money is missing, note the UTR/request ID and raise it with support. Also check with your bank/UPI app for any holding or limit issues.

  1. “Minimum withdrawal not met” or bonus locked

Only real winnings (after meeting any playthrough rules) are withdrawable. Keep playing until the eligible balance clears the threshold shown in the app.

  1. KYC / PAN / Aadhaar rejection

Blurry photos, name spelling differences, or PAN not linked to Aadhaar are frequent. Re-upload clear documents. Ensure your bank account name matches your KYC documents exactly.

  1. Withdrawal option missing or greyed out

Often means KYC is incomplete, daily limit reached, or the account has a temporary restriction. Complete verification or wait for the next daily reset.

Practical Tips for Smooth Teen Patti Stars Withdrawal in India

  • Finish KYC the same day you create the account.
  • Start with a small withdrawal (₹100–₹500) to test the rail.
  • Prefer UPI over bank transfer for speed.
  • For OTPs, make sure your registered mobile number is active. 
  • Avoid using someone else’s UPI or bank details.
  • Note every request ID and screenshot the confirmation.
  • Contact support only through the official in-app channel with clear details.
  • Play responsibly — set personal deposit and time limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum amount for teen patti stars withdrawal?

It varies by method and current app rules, commonly starting around ₹100–₹300 for UPI. Always check the live figure inside the wallet section.

Do I need KYC for every withdrawal?

Usually once. After successful verification, later withdrawals are faster, though large amounts may trigger extra checks.

Can I withdraw bonus money?

Generally no. Only winnings generated after meeting the stated wagering requirements become withdrawable.

Is there a fee?

Many platforms charge a small percentage or fixed fee on some methods. Before you confirm, the app displays it. 

What if support does not reply?

Use the official in-app chat or help section and keep records. For payment disputes that involve your bank or UPI, you can also raise a complaint through your bank’s dispute process or the National Consumer Helpline if needed.
