Core Teen Patti Games and Variations

The heart of teen patti stars games remains the classic three-card format, but the platform expands it with several popular twists.

Classic Teen Patti

Standard 52-card deck, usually 2–5 players. Everyone gets three cards face down. You can choose to play Seen (after checking) or Blind (without looking).  Hands rank from Trail (three of a kind) at the top, down through Pure Sequence, Sequence, Colour, Pair, and High Card. Betting builds the pot until players fold or show. This is the purest version most Indians recognise.

Joker / Wild Card variants

One or more cards act as jokers. This raises the chance of strong hands and changes the strategy—suddenly weaker starting cards become playable. Revolving or fixed jokers appear in different tables.

Muflis (Lowball)

Everything flips. The lowest hand wins. A trail of aces becomes weak, while low scattered cards gain value. It rewards different thinking and keeps tables lively.

AK47

Aces, Kings, 4s and 7s often carry special weight or act as wilds depending on the exact table rules. It creates more action and bigger swings.

999

Cards are valued so players try to get as close as possible to 999. Face cards usually count as zero and Ace as one. Quick, different, and popular with players who like number-based decisions.

Sudden Death and other fast modes

These cut the usual back-and-forth. Fewer rounds, higher intensity—ideal when you want a short session.

These variations keep the classic Teen Patti feel while giving regulars something new. Tables often show boot amounts clearly so you can choose stakes that suit your bankroll.

Other Major Games Available

Teen Patti Stars is not limited to three-card play. Here are the main additional titles commonly featured:

Indian Rummy

Thirteen-card (or sometimes shorter) format. Form pure sequences and sets, declare first. Pure sequence is usually mandatory. It rewards memory, observation, and discarding discipline. Many Indian players switch between Teen Patti and Rummy in the same sitting.

Dragon Tiger

Simple and fast. There are two cards dealt: one for Tiger and one for Dragon.  You bet which side will be higher, or on a tie. Low minimum bets make it accessible for beginners or casual play between heavier sessions.

7 Up Down

Dice-based. Predict whether the total of two dice will land above 7, below 7, or exactly 7. Quick rounds and clear odds. Good for players who want something lighter after intense card tables.

Poker / Texas-style variants (including Texas Cowboys)

Full poker hands with community cards or related formats. Appeals to players who already know Hold’em or want to expand beyond pure Teen Patti.

Some versions of the platform have also featured side or mini-games such as Black Red, Fruit Party-style options, Crash, or Car Roulette in the past. Availability can change with updates, so check the current lobby after downloading.
