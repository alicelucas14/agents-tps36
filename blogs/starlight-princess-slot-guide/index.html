Quick Stats for Starlight Princess

  • Provider: Pragmatic Play
  • Grid: 6 reels × 5 rows
  • Win style: Scatter pays (cluster) — 8 or more matching symbols anywhere
  • RTP: By default, it is 96.50%; depending on the casino, it might alternatively be 95.51% or 94.50%. 
  • Volatility: High
  • Max win: 5,000× your stake
  • Bet range: Typically from around ₹15–₹20 up to ₹7,000+ (or equivalent in other currencies; always check the in-game limits)
  • Hit frequency: Roughly in the low-to-mid 20% range

Always open the info panel inside the game to confirm the exact RTP your casino is running. The difference between 96.50% and 94.50% matters over time on a high-volatility title.

How Winning Works in Starlight Princess

Forget fixed paylines. Starlight Princess uses a pay-anywhere (scatter pays) system. Land 8 or more of the same symbol anywhere on the 30-position grid and you get a win. The payment increases with the number of matching symbols. 

The Tumble function activates after a victory. As long as new victories continue to emerge, winning symbols vanish and new ones fall from above. One paid spin can produce a chain of tumbles. There’s no fixed limit — it stops only when no new winning cluster appears.

Symbols range from lower-paying colourful gems to higher-paying celestial icons (sun, heart, moon, star). The dispersion is the princess herself. 

Key Features That Drive the Wins

Multiplier Hearts

These special winged or glowing heart symbols appear randomly during base game spins and tumbles. Each carries a multiplier from 2× up to 500×. After all tumbles finish on a spin, the values of every multiplier currently on the reels are added together and applied to the total win from that sequence.

Free Spins

Land 4 or more princess scatters to trigger 15 free spins.

  • Multipliers from winning tumbles are gathered into a running total that remains active for the duration of the bonus round, which is a significant change in multiplier behavior during free spins. 
  • Collect 3 or more scatters during free spins to retrigger +5 extra spins. This progressive multiplier build-up is where the biggest potential sits.

Ante Bet

You can increase your stake by 25% for a higher chance of landing the free spins feature (roughly double the frequency according to common player reports and game design notes). It increases the price per spin, however it’s useful if you want more bonus entries. 

Bonus Buy

Many versions let you buy the free spins feature directly for 100× your stake. Availability and exact cost depend on the casino and local regulations.
