How to Play Teen Patti on the Mobile App (Quick Refresher)

The core rules stay the same whether you play at home or on teen patti stars mobile:

  • Standard 52-card deck. Usually 3–6 players.
  • Everyone puts in the boot (ante) to start the pot.
  • Each player gets three face-down cards.
  • You can play blind (without looking) or seen (after looking). Blind bets are usually lower.
  • Betting moves clockwise. Options typically include fold (pack), call/chaal, or raise.
  • Best hand wins. Ranking from strongest to weakest: Trail/Set (three of a kind), Pure Sequence (straight flush), Sequence (straight), Color (flush), Pair, High Card.

Variants like Joker or Muflis change the ranking or add wild cards, so read the table rules before joining. The app usually shows hand rankings and current pot clearly so you can decide quickly even on a smaller screen.
