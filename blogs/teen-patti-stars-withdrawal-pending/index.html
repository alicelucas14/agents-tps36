Why Is the Withdrawal of Teen Patti Stars Still Unresolved? 

A “pending” status simply means the app has received your request but hasn’t released the money yet. Common reasons include:

  • Incomplete or mismatched KYC — Aadhaar, PAN, selfie, or bank details don’t match perfectly (name spelling, DOB, or photo quality issues are frequent).
  • First-time or large withdrawal — Extra security or manual review is triggered.
  • Banking / UPI delays — Name mismatch between your UPI handle/bank account and PAN, temporary bank outages, high traffic, weekends, or public holidays.
  • High transaction volume — Peak hours, festival periods, or post-bonus rushes slow things down.
  • Security or compliance checks — Especially for bigger amounts (TDS-related verification under Indian tax rules can also play a role on legitimate platforms).
  • Incorrect payment details — Wrong account number, UPI ID, or inactive bank account.

Avoid submitting the same withdrawal request multiple times while it’s still pending—this can create more delays or flag the account.

How Long Does a Teen Patti Stars Withdrawal Usually Take?

Processing times vary:

  • Small, verified UPI withdrawals on a fully KYC-approved account can clear in minutes to a few hours.
  • First withdrawals, larger amounts, or anything needing manual review often take 24–72 hours.
  • Bank transfers (IMPS/NEFT) can stretch longer during weekends, bank holidays, or high-load periods.

If it’s been more than 48–72 hours with no update and your KYC is complete, it’s time to act.

What To Do If Your Teen Patti Stars Withdrawal Is Pending

  1. Check the status inside the app Go to Wallet → Transaction History. Note the exact status, reference/transaction ID, amount, and timestamp. Take clear screenshots.
  2. Verify your KYC and payment details Confirm Aadhaar + PAN are approved and the name matches your bank/UPI exactly. Fix any “under review” or rejected documents first—this is the most common fix.
  3. Contact official support Use the in-app support, WhatsApp, or Telegram channels listed in the app (Teen Patti Stars has shared numbers like Telegram +91 73833 18961 and WhatsApp options in older FAQ pages). Provide:
    • Registered mobile number
    • Withdrawal ID / screenshots
    • Amount and date
    • KYC status Stay polite and factual. Follow up if you don’t hear back within 24 hours.
  4. Double-check the receiving account Make sure the UPI ID or bank account is active and in the exact same name as your KYC. A small name mismatch (e.g., “Rahul K” vs “Rahul Kumar”) can silently hold the payment.
  5. Wait through one full banking cycle After the app marks it “processing,” give the bank/UPI network T+1 time. Many delays clear once the payment rail settles.
  6. Escalate if needed (India-specific options)
    • Raise a dispute with your bank/UPI provider using the UTR if money left the app but never reached you.
    • For suspected fraud or non-responsive apps, report on cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930. Keep all screenshots and transaction records.
    • Consumer complaint platforms can also help document the case.

Quick Tips to Avoid Future Pending Issues

  • Complete full KYC (Aadhaar + PAN + bank) before your first real withdrawal.
  • Use the same name and mobile number everywhere—registration, KYC, UPI, bank.
  • Start with a small test withdrawal after KYC approval.
  • Prefer UPI during bank working hours (roughly 9 AM–5 PM IST) when possible.
  • Keep screenshots of every step and never share OTPs or passwords.

FAQ – Teen Patti Stars Withdrawal Pending

Why is my Teen Patti Stars withdrawal still pending after 24 hours?

Usually KYC review, name mismatch, or banking delay. Check status and contact support with your transaction ID.
