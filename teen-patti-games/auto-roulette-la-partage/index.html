What Makes La Partage Unique?

The La Partage rule is a game-changer for roulette enthusiasts. Originating in French casinos, this rule stipulates that if the ball lands on zero, players who have placed even-money bets (like red/black or odd/even) will receive half their stake back. This significantly reduces the house edge, making it a favorite among strategic players. Unlike the En Prison rule, which offers a second chance, La Partage provides immediate compensation, enhancing the player’s experience.
