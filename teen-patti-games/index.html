7 Up Down

7 Up Down is a simple yet engaging card game often played in casinos. Players place bets on whether the sum of two dice rolls will be above 7 (7 Up), below 7 (7 Down), or exactly 7. The game offers straightforward gameplay with quick rounds, making it a favorite among casual gamblers.

American Roulette

One well-liked variation of the traditional casino game is American roulette. It features a wheel with 38 pockets, including numbers 1-36, 0, and 00. The presence of the double zero increases the house edge compared to European Roulette. Players can place various bets, including straight-up numbers, splits, and outside bets like red/black or odd/even.
