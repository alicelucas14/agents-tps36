Understanding the Basics of Andar Bahar

Andar Bahar is fundamentally a prediction game. Both “Andar” (inside) and “Bahar” (outside) are the sides that will correspond to a chosen card from the deck. The game requires a standard deck of 52 cards and a dealer to facilitate the gameplay. The dealer’s role is pivotal, as they control the flow of the game and ensure fairness.

Traditional Rules of Andar Bahar

The game begins with the dealer placing a card face-up in the center. This card, known as the “joker” or “cut card,” determines the target for the round. Cards are then alternately dealt to the Andar and Bahar sides until a card matching the joker’s rank appears. The side receiving the matching card wins the round. The simplicity of these rules makes Andar Bahar accessible to players of all ages.
