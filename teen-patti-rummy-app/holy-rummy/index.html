Understanding the Basics of Rummy

Rummy, at its core, is a skill-based card game that requires strategy, observation, and tactical moves. The primary objective is to arrange cards into valid sets or sequences, outmaneuvering opponents. In Holy Rummy, these traditional rules stay intact, creating a familiar but exhilarating environment for enthusiasts.

Types of Rummy Games in Holy Rummy

  1. Points Rummy: A fast-paced variant where each point has a designated value, and players compete to score the least points.
  2. Deals Rummy: Here, players play a set number of deals, with the highest scorer at the end emerging victorious.
  3. Pool Rummy: Players contribute to a “pool,” and the aim is to reach zero points by forming sequences quickly, with the last remaining player winning the pool.
