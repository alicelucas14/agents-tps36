Basic Rules of Rummy

Rummy is a game of skill and strategy. The basic objective is to create sets and sequences of cards. A sequence is made up of three or more cards of the same suit that are arranged consecutively. Three or more identically ranked cards from various suits make up a set.

Card Discarding Strategies

Discarding cards strategically is crucial in rummy. You need to discard cards that are less likely to help you form sequences or sets.

Winning the Game

To win a game of rummy, you must meld all your cards into valid sequences and sets. Once you’ve melded all your cards, you declare your hand and win the game.
