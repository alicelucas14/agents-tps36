Understanding the Rules of Rummy Star

Basics of Rummy

The key is to use the cards you have in your hand to form valid sets and sequences. Declare your hand before your opponent to win the game.

Key Terminology

  • Pure Sequence: Without the use of a joker, a sequence was created.
  • Impure Sequence: At least one joker appears in this sequence.
  • Joker Card: A wildcard that can substitute for other cards to form sequences or sets.

Scoring System

Points are assigned based on card values, and minimizing your score is essential to outsmart your opponents.

Types of Rummy Games on Rummy Star

Deals Rummy
