Teen Patti Gold Rules and Gameplay

Teen Patti Gold follows the same rules as classic Teen Patti, where players receive three cards, aiming to achieve the best hand ranking. The game is won by the player with the highest-ranking combination or through clever bluffing to make opponents fold. Hand ranks include high card, pairs, flush, sequence, and trio, with the trio being the highest rank in standard Teen Patti Gold.
